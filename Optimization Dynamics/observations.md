# Observations — Optimization Dynamics

## Stable Learning Rate
- Loss decreases smoothly
- Converges to minimum
- Weights remain controlled

## High Learning Rate
- Loss oscillates or diverges
- Training becomes unstable
- Weight values explode

## Low Learning Rate
- Loss decreases very slowly
- Training inefficient
- May get stuck before optimal point

## Weight Distribution
- Stable training → centered distribution
- Unstable training → wide or extreme spread

## Weight Norm Growth
- Stable → gradual increase
- Unstable → rapid spikes

## Key Insight
Learning rate controls stability, convergence speed, and overall training behavior.
