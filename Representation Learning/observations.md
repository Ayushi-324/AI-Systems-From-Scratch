# Observations — Representation Learning

## Raw Space
- Data is non-linearly separable
- Classes are entangled

## Hidden Layer 1
- Initial structure begins to form
- Partial separation observed

## Hidden Layer 2
- Clear clustering emerges
- Data becomes linearly separable

## Decision Boundary
- Model learns non-linear transformation
- Boundary aligns with data geometry

## Distance Transformation
- Average distances increase in feature space
- Classes become more distinguishable

## Key Insight
Neural networks do not just classify — they reshape data geometry to enable classification.
